FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What services does BridanX Tech LLC provide?

BridanX provides website development, application development, digital platform development, custom software, API integrations and automation solutions.

Can you build a custom website for my business?

Yes. Website projects can be designed around your brand, audience, business goals and required functionality.

Can you build a complete digital platform?

Yes. Platform projects can include user accounts, dashboards, databases, business workflows, APIs and other required functionality.

Do you develop mobile applications?

Application development can be planned according to the required platforms, features, users and technical requirements.

Can you integrate third-party services?

Yes. API and integration work can connect appropriate external services with your website, application or platform.

How do I start a project?

Contact BridanX through email, phone or WhatsApp and describe what you want to build. The project can then be scoped around your requirements.
